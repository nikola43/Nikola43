## 🌮 A Little About Me

> *"The typical friend who believes that with an Arduino can solve ANY technological problem... and now also with Solana!"* 

Hey! I'm **Paulo Soares**, a passionate builder, tinkerer, and blockchain developer from **Sevilla, Andalusia 🇪🇸**. 

Since childhood, I've been the kid who disassembles everything with screws, wires, batteries, and LEDs. Now I'm doing the same thing... but with smart contracts and DeFi protocols! 🤯

**From Arduino to Web3 - Here's my journey:**
- 🔌 Started with **Arduino & IoT** projects
- 💻 Evolved into **Full-Stack Development**
- 🌐 Dived deep into **Web3 & Blockchain**
- 🤖 Now building **Solana bots, MEV systems & DeFi tools**

Lucky me - I get to make a living doing what I love! 🎉
